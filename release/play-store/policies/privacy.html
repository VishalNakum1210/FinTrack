FinTrack Privacy Policy

Updated 31 October 2026. FinTrack is created by Vishal Nakum. Contact: daku.9705@gmail.com.

Information and use

We process your name, phone account identifier, email, optional profile address, income/expense entries, friend names/numbers, split/trip records and feedback (rating, type, message, contact email and timestamp) for account management, ledger synchronization and support. Email is required at registration but is not a verified recovery address. Friend details are entered manually; FinTrack does not read device contacts.

Services and security

Google Firebase Authentication processes account credentials. Firebase Realtime Database stores account, financial and feedback records. Firebase also processes technical service/connection data including resource identifiers and IP addresses. Developer/authorized operators may access records for support, security and operation. TLS protects transport and UID-based rules restrict cloud access. This is not end-to-end encryption. Firebase caches financial records locally; profile/session and pending draft metadata may use secure storage. Protect your device.

The reviewed app integrates no advertising or analytics product and does not sell financial data. You choose export recipients; exported PDF/CSV/JSON files are unencrypted. Third-party destinations receive the files you select.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while your account is in use. Profile > Delete Account removes financial roots, feedback and profile contact details before authentication deletion. A minimal phone account ID, UID and deletion marker remain for safe retries and ownership protection, without automatic expiry currently implemented. This marker may be replaced after clean re-registration. Provider backup/log retention depends on Firebase configuration; contact support for specific requests. Exports and records independently entered by other users are not recalled. If deletion fails, reconnect and retry or contact support.

Your choices

Edit profile information, export available records or request account deletion. To request access, correction or deletion without reinstalling, use our account deletion page or email support. Never send your password. We verify ownership independently before account actions. Material privacy changes will be disclosed through an app update or notice.
