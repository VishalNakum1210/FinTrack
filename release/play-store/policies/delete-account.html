Delete your FinTrack account

FinTrack is created by Vishal Nakum. You can request deletion without installing or reopening the app.

Request by email

Email daku.9705@gmail.com to request account deletion. Include your FinTrack account phone ID and a way to reply. Do not include passwords, OTPs, bank statements or government identity documents in the initial request. The developer will reply to establish independent ownership verification before deleting anything; knowing a phone number or profile email alone is not sufficient.

Delete inside the app

When signed in, open Profile > Delete Account > Delete Permanently and confirm your password. Use connectivity and wait for confirmation. If an error occurs, retry when connected or contact support.

Data removed and retained

Your financial history, friends/trips, feedback and profile contact fields are removed before Firebase Auth deletion. A minimal phone account ID, UID and deletion marker remain for ownership/security and safe retries, currently without automatic expiry; it can be replaced after clean re-registration. Contact support about this retention. Firebase provider backup/log retention needs case-specific confirmation. Previously exported files and independently entered records in other accounts are not recalled. Export your own records first if needed.
