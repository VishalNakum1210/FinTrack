## Full description

FinTrack helps you keep a personal record of income, expenses and shared bills.

• Record cash and online income or expenses.
• Browse and filter your passbook and edit individual entries.
• Keep private friend ledgers and split shared bills with exact-paise calculations.
• View category summaries and financial charts based on the records you enter.
• Export available records as PDF statements, CSV spreadsheets or JSON archives using your device's print and share tools.
• Manage your profile and request deletion of your account from Profile.

FinTrack is created by Vishal Nakum. It is a recordkeeping tool: it does not connect to bank accounts, hold money, make payments or provide professional financial advice. Friend entries are your own records; they do not verify a debt or settle it with another person.

An account and internet connection are required for sign-in and synchronization. Some cached data is available offline; server confirmation requires connectivity. Phone numbers act as account IDs and are not verified by SMS. Profile email cannot reset a password. Support-assisted account recovery requires independent verification and cannot be guaranteed.

Exports are unencrypted files: choose where to save and share them carefully. JSON export is an archive; this version has no in-app restore/import. Review the privacy policy for Firebase processing, account deletion and retention of minimal security markers.
